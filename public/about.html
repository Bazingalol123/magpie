Magpie turns web research into living collections

Magpie helps people compare products, apartments, jobs, recipes, articles, and other things they find across the web.

Capture a useful piece of a page with the Chrome extension. Magpie preserves the source evidence, organizes the capture into a structured Collection, and keeps meaningful changes visible over time.

How it works

  1. Capture an element, selection, image, link, or page.
  2. Let Magpie propose the right structure and Collection.
  3. Review uncertain captures instead of accepting invented facts.
  4. Compare saved Items and refresh supported sources when they change.
