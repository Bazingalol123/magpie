Privacy overview

This page is a plain-language product overview, not a substitute for terms or legal advice. It describes the current product behavior and will be expanded into formal policy text before a public launch.

What Magpie stores

When you use Magpie, your workspace can contain captures, source URLs, bounded text or HTML context, screenshots, structured Items, Collections, review decisions, and source-change history.

Who can read workspace data

Dashboard reads are authenticated and owner-scoped. The Chrome extension is write-only: its pairing token can submit captures but cannot read your workspace.

What you control

You can review uncertain captures and delete saved Items through the dashboard. Sources that require a login are shown as blocked rather than replaced with guessed data.
