Why sourdough starters go dormant

A starter left unfed in the refrigerator for two or three weeks will usually still be alive underneath a layer of dark liquid. The liquid, sometimes called hooch, is a sign of hunger rather than spoilage, and a starter that smells sharply of alcohol or vinegar can almost always be brought back with two or three regular feedings at room temperature.

Discard all but a tablespoon of the starter, then feed it equal parts flour and water by weight. Repeat every twelve hours until it doubles in volume within six hours of feeding.
